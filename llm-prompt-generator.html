🎯 Pro Tips for Maximum AI Adherence
  • Be specific with the role: Instead of "expert", say "senior cybersecurity expert with CISSP certification"
  • Use "You are" not "Act as": Makes AI internalize the role deeper
  • Set clear boundaries: Tell AI what NOT to do as well as what to do
  • Reinforce the persona: Reference the role in constraints (e.g., "As a senior architect, you always...")
  • Request thinking out loud: Asking AI to show reasoning keeps it on track
  • Specify output format precisely: Structured outputs reduce hallucination
  • Add examples of tone: Show don't just tell (include a sample sentence)
  • Use system messages: For ChatGPT/Claude, put role in system message when possible
